## Runtime services

Nimbus fetches, without bundling:

- **Open-Meteo** (https://open-meteo.com/) — weather fields and forecasts. CC BY 4.0.
- **EOX::Maps** (https://maps.eox.at/) — Sentinel-2 cloudless, Terrain Light and OpenStreetMap raster tiles. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors and others; rendering © EOX. Attribution and a link back are required.
- **Esri ArcGIS basemaps** (World Street Map, World Imagery, World Topo Map, World Boundaries and Places) — © Esri and contributors. Esri basemap terms apply. This project uses the public tile services for a free, non-commercial community app with on-screen attribution; review the terms before any commercial distribution.
- **adsb.lol** — community ADS-B positions. Free service; attribution.
- **OpenSky Network** — world aircraft snapshot. Non-commercial terms; attribution.
- **NASA EONET** — tropical systems. Public domain / NASA.
- **NOAA NHC** — active storms. US Government work.
- **OpenStreetMap** — map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
